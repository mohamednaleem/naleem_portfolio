01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Twelve years of building
things that work.

I’m a Senior Full Stack Developer with 12+ years of experience across front-end, full-stack, and API development. I build responsive enterprise web applications, REST APIs, dashboards, and customer-facing portals with Angular, React.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and Oracle.

Today I work as a MERN stack developer at Cognizant on DLL One Source, a banking application, shipping through Azure DevOps CI/CD with GitHub Copilot-assisted development. Before that, I led front-end delivery for Abbott’s healthcare platforms as a Technical Lead at HCL.

I care about clean, maintainable code, thoughtful code reviews, and clear communication with clients and teammates.
